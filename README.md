## 📌 Project Overview

A comprehensive **Business Analysis and Product Documentation project** for the Falaq Food custom **ERP/CRM and E-commerce Platform**.

The project focuses on understanding business problems, customer and internal user pain points, operational challenges, system workflows, business requirements, and potential improvements.

The analysis translates business needs into structured and actionable product requirements through **Business Requirements Documents (BRDs), Business Logic, User Flows, Acceptance Criteria, and Development Backlog Tickets**.

**Application:** Falaq Food Custom ERP/CRM & E-commerce Platform  
**Role:** Business Analyst / Product Analyst  
**Focus:** Business Analysis + Requirements Engineering + Product Documentation  
**Documentation:** BRDs + Business Logic + User Flows + Backlog  
**Documentation Tools:** Confluence + Google Docs  
**UI/UX & User Flows:** Figma  
**AI-Assisted Analysis:** Claude AI  
**Research:** Competitor & Market Research  
**Collaboration:** Development + Operations + SEO + UI/UX + Management

# 👨‍💼 Business Analysis

## 🔍 Problem & Pain Point Discovery

A major part of the analysis process was understanding **why a feature was needed before defining how it should work**.

The analysis focused on identifying problems across customers, internal users, operations, data visibility, workflows, access control, usability, automation, and reporting.

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| 👤 Customer Pain Points | Customer-facing issues and friction |
| 👨‍💼 User Pain Points | Agent and internal user workflow difficulties |
| ⚙️ Operational Issues | Bottlenecks and inefficient processes |
| 📊 Data Visibility | Missing or insufficient business information |
| 🔄 Workflow Gaps | Manual, repetitive, or inefficient processes |
| 🔐 Access Issues | Incorrect or unnecessary data visibility |
| 🖥️ Usability Issues | Difficult or unclear user interactions |
| 🤖 Automation Opportunities | Processes that could be system-driven |
| 📈 Reporting Gaps | Missing operational and business insights |
